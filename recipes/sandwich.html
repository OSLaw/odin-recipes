Sandwich

Description

A sandwich is an easy meal you can make for breakfast or dinner.
All you need is bread, lettuce, tomato, and any meat of your choice if you are not vegetarian or vegan.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 tomato fruit
  • some lettuce
  • meat (optional)
  • mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Slice your tomato and lettuce
  2. Spread mayonnaise on both slices of your bread
  3. Add the sliced tomato and lettuce
  4. Add your meat strips
  5. Merge the bunch and munch!
